About

I build, test, and explain models in financial applications.

I work across machine learning, artificial intelligence, accounting, finance, and cloud systems. My focus is on how models are built, tested, governed, and explained — especially when they affect financial decisions.

Credentials

Associate Professor in ML/AI

Academic work in machine learning, artificial intelligence, and model-based decision systems.

Professional accounting and finance background for understanding how models affect reporting, risk, and business decisions.

Cloud architecture knowledge for understanding how data and model systems are implemented in practice.

How I teach

I do not teach models as magic or certainty. I teach them as structured arguments: inputs, assumptions, calculations, tests, outputs, and limits.
